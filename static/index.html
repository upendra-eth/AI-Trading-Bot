ℹ️ How Paper Trading Works

The engine runs scheduled cycles, fetching live data from Yahoo Finance, retraining the ML models, and reading real-time news headlines for each stock in the watchlist.
• Position Sizing: Each BUY allocates 20% of available cash. Only one open position per symbol at a time.
• Entry Signal: XGBoost(35%) + LSTM(35%) + FinBERT(30%) ensemble must agree on BUY above threshold.
• Exit Signal: Ensemble must generate SELL signal while a position is OPEN for that symbol.
• Risk: This is completely simulated paper trading. No real money is involved.
